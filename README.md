This application will assist scouts who have been appointed "Grub Master" for a particular camping trip. First, this is, and hopefully always will be a work in progress. Conceptualized and made a ticket item for my Wood Badge Adult Leadership Course, 15 - 514 - 25. As an adult leader for several scout troops over the years, there has always been a common theme when it comes to scout meal planning. What is the quickest, and what uses the least amount of dishes to be washed. These are not bad goals when meal planning, but the result usually ends up being Bubba Burgers for dinner on Saturday night and Pop Tarts for breakfast on Sunday morning. Contrast this with the adult leaders who take it as a challenge to make good, cheap food. The original idea was to have a cookbook of sorts to assist scouts in preparing their meal plan and prerequisite shopping list, and subsequent duty roster. This is actually nothing new, I actually ran across one such cook book when assisting clean-up of an old storage area. Many of those recipes made it into this application. The problem with the cookbook idea is that it takes up space. Given that many troops, these days, are essentially being gifted a place to meet that does not necessarily come with storage for cookbooks, or much of anything else, I thought a digital option would be great. “Yeah, an app! The only problem is I don't know how to code. But this is the modern age and AI can do things mere mortal men can not. So, with my apologies to real coders everywhere, this is what Claude and I came up with. Mostly, it is pretty obvious. A bunch of recipes, a tool to add more. A page where you add the type of camping (which actually is the type of cooking) in the number of scouts and adult leaders if they are going to be cooked for. I added carve outs for vegetarians, and nut allergies. Pick which meals you are doing, and whammo!, a list of recipes is generated. Select which ones you want, and a shopping list taking into the number of needed servings is generated. Additionally cooking instructions are provided. The is a feature that will allow the user to upload recipes. In the future, I hope to offer different stores based on geographical area where the ingredients may be found the cheapest to align with scouts being thrifty.
